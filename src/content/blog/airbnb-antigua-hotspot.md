## The US Market Problem

The US Airbnb market has become increasingly saturated. Competition is fierce, regulations are tightening, and yields are declining. Many investors are looking for alternatives.

## Why Antigua Guatemala?

Antigua Guatemala offers everything an Airbnb investor dreams of:
- **Growing tourism**: Guatemala closed 2025 with 3.36 million international visitors, up 11% on 2024 (INGUAT), and Antigua remains the country's most-visited destination
- **Limited supply**: The historic center has a fixed number of properties
- **High demand**: Year-round tourism with peak seasons
- **Low operating costs**: Property management, maintenance, and utilities are affordable

## Real Numbers

Based on actual properties in Antigua:
- **Average nightly rate**: $80-150 for a colonial home
- **Occupancy rate**: 60-80% annually
- **Annual rental income**: $18,000-45,000
- **Net yield**: 8-15% after expenses

## Best Areas for Airbnb

1. **Antigua Centro**. Highest demand, premium rates
2. **Paseo del Obispo**. Quiet, popular with families
3. **San Miguel Dueñas**. Growing area, better value

## What the 2026 Numbers Actually Look Like

The tourism case for Antigua got stronger, not weaker. INGUAT reported 3,361,843 non-resident visitors to Guatemala in 2025, an 11% increase over 2024, and Antigua Guatemala held its place as the country's most-visited destination. More visitors means more nights booked.

Short-term rental data for 2026 is more sober than the headline yields, and that is a good thing. A typical Antigua listing now books at roughly 55% occupancy, with a median nightly rate near $83 and strong performers averaging $138 to $148 a night. Semana Santa and the December holidays push the best colonial homes above 80% occupancy and rates 25% to 40% higher than the yearly median.

What that means for a buyer: a well-located colonial home at $120 a night and 65% occupancy grosses about $28,000 a year before costs. The 8% to 15% net yield still exists, but it comes from buying well and running lean, not from assuming every month is high season.
