## Why Antigua Guatemala?

Antigua Guatemala has become one of the most popular destinations for expats in Central America. With its spring-like weather, affordable cost of living, vibrant community, and stunning colonial architecture, it's easy to see why.

## Visa Requirements

Most nationalities can enter Guatemala visa-free for up to 90 days. After that, you can extend for another 90 days at the immigration office. For longer stays, you'll need to apply for temporary residency.

## Finding Your First Home

The rental market in Antigua offers something for every budget:
- **Budget**: $350-600/month for a basic apartment
- **Mid-range**: $550-1,100/month for a furnished colonial home
- **Premium**: $1,100-2,500/month for a luxury property

Prices rose with demand through 2026; the ranges above reflect the market at the end of the year.

## Cost of Living

Antigua offers an incredibly affordable lifestyle:
- **Rent**: $300-1,500/month
- **Groceries**: $150-300/month
- **Dining out**: $5-15 per meal
- **Transportation**: $1-3 per ride
- **Healthcare**: $30-50 per doctor visit

## Getting Around

Antigua is a walkable city, most things are within a 15-minute walk. For longer trips, tuk-tuks cost $1-2 and Uber is available for trips to Guatemala City.

## What Rent Actually Costs in 2026

Rental prices have drifted up with demand, and the gap between the center and just outside it is the single most useful number for a new arrival. As of late 2026, a one-bedroom apartment inside the historic center averages about $877 a month, while the same flat just outside it averages about $523, a discount of roughly 40% for a short walk. A three-bedroom house in the center averages about $1,900.

Those figures move with the quetzal, so treat them as a guide rather than a contract. The practical reading is simple: pay the premium if you want to live inside the colonial grid, and save 30% to 40% if you are happy to walk in.

## Where to Live When You Arrive

- **Historic center:** the highest rent, and everything within a 15-minute walk. This is the Antigua most people picture.
- **Grid edges (San Felipe, Santa Ana):** 10 to 20 minutes on foot from the center, with rent 30% to 40% lower.
- **Villages (Jocotenango, San Pedro Las Huertas, Ciudad Vieja):** quieter, better value, and easier with a car or tuk-tuk.

## Frequently asked questions

**How much does it cost to rent a one-bedroom apartment in Antigua Guatemala?**
A furnished one-bedroom averages about $877 a month inside the historic center and about $523 outside it as of late 2026, a discount of roughly 40% for a short walk. Prices drift with the quetzal, so reconvert before you sign.

**Do I need a car to live in Antigua Guatemala?**
Not if you live in or near the center. Antigua is about one square mile and walkable, with tuk-tuks at $1 to $2 a ride. A car helps if you settle in one of the surrounding villages or drive to Guatemala City often.
