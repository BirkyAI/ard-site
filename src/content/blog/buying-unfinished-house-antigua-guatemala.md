An unfinished house is often the cheapest way into Antigua Guatemala's colonial grid. The same street can hold a bare shell asking around $250,000 and a finished house asking $650,000, and almost all of that gap is restoration, not postcode. But you are not buying a house: you are buying a construction site, a permit file and a set of risks.

## What counts as an unfinished house in Antigua Guatemala?

Obra gris means the structure only: footings, walls, sometimes a ring beam, no plaster and in places no roof. Obra blanca means the walls are plastered and the roof is on, with no kitchen, no bathroom fittings and no floors. You will also see a house finished except for one wing, a colonial ruin with original walls and no roof, and a bare-block shell abandoned by a builder.

## Why do unfinished houses come up for sale here?

For ordinary reasons. An owner dies and the heirs, abroad or in Guatemala City, would rather sell the draw than finish it. A foreign owner runs out of money or leaves mid-project. Some, though, are cheap because the slab was poured in the wrong place, the permit was never issued, or the title carries a problem. Treat a low price as a question before you treat it as a bargain.

## What are you actually buying?

| What stage | What exists | What to verify first | The main risk |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| Bare structure (obra gris) | Footings, walls, sometimes a ring beam | Walls plumb, footing sized for your plans, permit file valid | You inherit someone else's structural mistake |
| Roofed shell (obra blanca) | Plastered walls, roof on, no finishes | Water, electricity and drainage connections and the cost to extend them | Services cost more to bring in than finishes |
| Colonial ruin | Original adobe or calicanto walls, no roof | An engineer's view, plus the CNPAG position on the facade | A ruin is a rebuild, and the heritage file is the slow part |

## How do you price an unfinished house?

Not from the asking price, or the builder's optimistic quote. Price it as finished value minus completion cost minus a margin for what you cannot see. Restoration here runs $150 to $400 per square metre depending on how much of it is structural, a light cosmetic finish on a sound shell sits at $10,000 to $25,000, and a full renovation of a colonial house runs $30,000 to $120,000.

So a shell asking $250,000 where finished houses ask $650,000 is not a $400,000 saving. Deduct restoration, a roof, the wet rooms and a 15 to 20 percent contingency, then a margin for the surprises behind the plaster. Do that arithmetic on square metres, never on the story.

## What should you check before you make an offer?

- **The permit file, and its date.** A licencia de construcción does not last forever, and work done after it lapses can be stopped or fined, as our [guide to building permits](/blog/building-permits-antigua-guatemala/) sets out.
- **The structure.** Walk the walls with an engineer. Plumb walls, a real ring beam and a footing sized for the next level are what you are paying for, and a study runs Q3,500 to Q12,000.
- **The services and the rain.** A live water meter, a tinaco, a drenaje or fosa séptica, and an electrical service that matches your plans. An unroofed shell also funnels the May to October rain into the walls ([drainage and rainwater](/blog/drainage-antigua-guatemala-homes/)).
- **The money owed on it.** A fresh certificación registral from the Registro de la Propiedad, plus IUSI and municipal certificates ([checking for liens](/blog/property-liens-debts-guatemala/)). The unfinished stage must be described at the deed and registered at the catastro, and an open site can be hard to insure, so confirm both before you pay a deposit.

## What does it cost to finish a house here?

These are 2026 estimates for a mid-range finish, converted at about Q7.7 to the dollar.

| Work | Typical cost (USD) |
|:--|:--|
| Kitchen, cabinets and appliances | $5,000 to $15,000 |
| One bathroom, complete | $2,500 to $8,000 |
| New clay tile roof, including structure | $8,000 to $20,000 |
| Electrical rewiring | $3,000 to $8,000 |
| Plumbing | $2,000 to $6,000 |
| Floor tile throughout | $3,000 to $10,000 |
| Interior paint and facade restoration | $2,000 to $6,000 |
| Courtyard and garden | $1,500 to $5,000 |

Add 15 to 20 percent for contingency. Labour is paid by the day: a maestro de obra runs Q300 to Q500 and an albañil Q150 to Q300. On a shell that needs no structural work, finishing typically lands between $400 and $900 per square metre, the lower half of the $800 to $1,500 a square metre a complete new build costs. If the structure needs work too, budget the full renovation figure instead.

## Can you get a mortgage on an unfinished house in Guatemala?

Practically, no. Banks lend 60 to 70 percent of an appraised value and appraise a finished, habitable house, so an unfinished property is hard to appraise and hard to insure. Most buyers pay cash or refinance at home, then refinance locally once it is complete. Budget 5 to 7 percent of the price for closing costs, and see our guide to [Guatemala mortgages for foreign buyers](/blog/guatemala-mortgage-financing-foreigners/).

## How long does it take, and what does it cost you while you wait?

Finishing the second half of a house commonly takes four to eight months; a full build runs eight to fourteen, and permits add four to ten weeks. While it stands unfinished it earns nothing, and IUSI, insurance, security and a caretaker still run.

## Is an unfinished house a good investment in Antigua?

It can be the best-value route in the market, and the one that produces the widest margins here, as our guide to [renovating for profit](/blog/renovating-for-profit-antigua-guatemala/) explains. The deals that work share a sound structure, a live permit and a clean title. The ones to walk away from share an unpermitted build, a dispute with an heir, or a structural mistake nobody will admit to. Get an independent [inspection](/blog/home-inspection-antigua-guatemala/) and a survey before you sign.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is an unfinished house in Antigua Guatemala cheaper?

Usually, yes. But once you add restoration at $150 to $400 per square metre, a roof, the wet rooms and a contingency, the discount is far smaller.

### Can a foreigner buy an unfinished house in Guatemala?

Yes. A foreigner buys here on the same terms as a citizen, with no residency needed. What matters is the permit file and the title, not your passport.

### Can you get a mortgage on an unfinished house?

Rarely. Banks lend against a house they can appraise, so an unfinished property is usually a cash purchase or financed from abroad.
