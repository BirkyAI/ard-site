Plenty of Antigua Guatemala rentals change hands with a tenant already inside. In practice, a sale does not wipe out an existing lease: the buyer generally steps into the landlord's shoes and takes the property subject to the terms the current owner signed. That is good news if you want income from day one, and a complication if you want to move in. Either way, the work happens before you sign, by verifying the lease, the rent, the deposit and any arrears, then deciding whether you are buying the tenant or buying vacant possession.

This guide covers the buyer's side of an occupied rental, and it pairs with our guide to [tenant and landlord rights in Guatemala](/blog/landlord-tenant-rights-guatemala/) for the rules that apply once you own the place.

## Does a lease survive when a rental property is sold in Antigua Guatemala?

Under the Guatemalan Civil Code, a transfer of ownership does not by itself cancel a residential lease. The new owner substitutes the old landlord and inherits both the rights and the obligations: collect the agreed rent, honour the term, return the deposit at the end, and handle necessary repairs. A lease that was registered makes that substitution enforceable against third parties, which is one reason registration is worth the paperwork on a long fixed-term contract.

A verbal lease is legally valid too, which is where buyers get caught. If the terms were never written down, the buyer inherits an arrangement whose details are disputed by memory. That is not a reason to walk away automatically, but it is a reason to price the risk and to get the tenant to confirm the terms in writing at closing.

## What should you check before buying an occupied rental?

The due diligence on a tenanted property looks different from an empty one, because part of the value sits in a contract. Work through these items in order.

| What to check | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| The original signed lease | Confirms the rent, currency, term end date, escalation clause and notice period |
| Rent receipts for the last 12 months | Shows whether the tenant actually pays, and on time |
| Deposit amount and who holds it | You inherit the refund obligation at move-out |
| Certified inventory (acta) if furnished | Protects you from a furniture and condition dispute later |
| Registry search (folio real) and IUSI status | Confirms the seller owns it and that taxes are current |
| Whether the lease is registered | A registered lease binds a buyer; an unregistered one lives between the original parties |

If the tenant will not cooperate with a viewing or a written confirmation of terms, treat that as information, not an obstacle to smooth over.

## How does a sitting tenant change the price?

Two buyers price the same house differently. An investor buys the income stream, so the number is set by net yield: annual rent minus expenses, divided by the purchase price and closing costs. A buyer who wants to live there buys vacant possession, so the price should carry a discount for the months of lost rent, the legal cost of ending the tenancy, and the risk that the tenant contests it.

The most common mistake is comparing a tenanted property against empty listings without adjusting. An occupied house at a market rent of Q8,000 a month leased at Q5,000 is not a small problem: that gap is locked for the rest of the term. Read our breakdown of [rental income potential in Antigua](/blog/rental-income-potential-antigua-guatemala/) before you model the yield, because gross rent overstates it once vacancy, management and repairs are counted.

## What if you want the property empty?

You cannot buy an occupied rental and expect to move in next week. Three routes exist, in order of cost and speed:

- Wait out the lease term, which is the cleanest and usually the cheapest option.
- Negotiate an early exit with the tenant, a mutual agreement with compensation, often called a cash-for-keys arrangement.
- Go to court. A contested eviction (juicio de desalojo) runs through a judge, takes months, and the tenant has the right to respond. Lock changes, removed belongings and cut utilities are illegal, so a buyer who tries to shortcut this inherits a lawsuit, not a house.

If your plan depends on living there within a set date, buy vacant or budget six months of flexibility.

## What are the real risks of buying a tenanted property?

Be honest about these before you commit:

- Under-market rent locked for the full term, which drags the yield down and limits your resale audience.
- Informal leases tracked on WhatsApp, with no written term or deposit record.
- A tenant who stops paying mid-term, leaving you with the same months-long court route.
- Limited inspection, because furniture and belongings hide finishes, damp and damage.
- The deposit you owe back at move-out, which is a real liability even though it never touched your account.
- Utility arrears in a tenant's name that become a fight at handover.

## How does an occupied rental affect financing and closing?

Most purchases in Antigua are cash or equity-funded, because local mortgages for foreign buyers are limited. If you are using a lender, expect it to ask whether the property is occupied and to want either vacant possession or proof of the rent. Plan for that early.

At closing, three things need a home in writing. Confirm that IUSI and municipal charges are paid to the date of sale, and that utility meters and accounts transfer cleanly. Record the deposit as a transferred liability in the escritura or an annex, with the amount and the tenant's acknowledgment. Prorate the current month's rent between seller and buyer. Get the tenant's signature on a notice of sale and a confirmation of the lease terms, and if the lease is registrable, register it.

## Six checks before you commit

1. Obtain the original lease and a copy of the tenant's identification, and read every clause.
2. Ask for twelve months of rent receipts and the deposit ledger.
3. Pull the folio real and confirm IUSI is current.
4. Inspect with the tenant present, and photograph every room including under furniture.
5. Have a lawyer draft the sale notice and the tenant's acknowledgment before closing.
6. Model the yield on net rent, not gross, and add the cost of vacant possession if that is your goal.

An occupied rental can be the smoothest purchase in Antigua, with income arriving the month you take title and a tenant who already cares for the house. It can also be a slow, expensive lesson in unverified paperwork. The difference is almost always the file you build before the deposit leaves your account.
