## About This Property

A bright, newly finished home with a colonial feel in the exclusive Hacienda San Juan gated community, about 15 minutes from the historic center of Antigua on the road to Alotenango. The property is carefully designed for comfortable everyday living, with generous spaces, fine cedar wood details and a strong connection to the outdoors throughout.

- **4 bedrooms**, each with its own private bathroom
- **1 bedroom on the ground floor** and 3 bedrooms upstairs
- **Living room and dining room**
- **Kitchen** with a granite island and plenty of storage
- **Front and back gardens**
- **Parking** for 2 cars, one space at the front plus an extra spot on the 6 metre strip in front of the house
- **Laundry area**
- **Terrace**
- **Fine cedar wood details** in the doors, trim and ceiling beams
- **298 m² of construction** on a 10 × 18 metre lot

The asking price is US$475,000 plus taxes. Monthly HOA maintenance is Q400.

## Location

Hacienda San Juan is a private residential community on the south side of the Antigua valley, a short drive from the town center while staying set apart from the bustle. The setting offers views toward the surrounding volcanoes, easy access to everyday services, and the peace of a low density, controlled-access development.
