## About This Property

Villa Real is a colonial contemporary home with stunning views of the volcanoes, in a privileged spot just minutes from Antigua Guatemala. This is "the colonial gem" of the area, a residence that blends the charm of traditional architecture with modern comfort, and it is expected to be ready in about four weeks.

The design uses a subtle and elegant mix of translucent materials and enormous windows that both separate and connect the interior with the outside, making the most of the views of the three volcanoes. A living room on the second level sits in front of a large terrace, designed to keep those magical views from the residential front row.

Each bedroom includes its own walk-in closet and bathroom, and the master bedroom features the celebrated colonial coffered ceiling. The home is finished with a pool, an elegant pergola ideal for outdoor gatherings, a cozy fireplace for special moments, and two generous gardens that provide privacy and contact with nature. There is parking for up to four vehicles.

- **3 bedrooms**, each with a walk-in closet and private bathroom
- **Master bedroom** with the celebrated colonial coffered ceiling
- **Living and dining areas**, with the second-level living room facing the terrace
- **Pool**
- **Pergola** for outdoor gatherings
- **Fireplace**
- **Two gardens**
- **Ample natural light** through large windows with volcano views
- **Pet friendly**
- **24-hour security and private surveillance**
- **Parking** for up to 4 vehicles
- **440 m² of construction** on two levels

The asking price is US$660,000 plus taxes. The home is expected to be ready in approximately four weeks.

## Location

Villa Real sits in an exclusive residential community a short drive from the historic center of Antigua Guatemala. In addition to its green areas and sweeping volcano views, the community offers a private social club, a gym, a football pitch and round-the-clock security.
