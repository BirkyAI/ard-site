## About This Property

Villa San Juan is a contemporary colonial home, finished with fine natural cedar wood, in a private residential community just a few minutes from the historic center of Antigua Guatemala. It combines colonial character with modern comfort, with clean lines, high ceilings and a strong connection to the outdoors throughout.

Inside, the layout feels open and light. The living, dining and kitchen areas flow together in a space finished with exposed cedar beams and certified Brazilian granite. Large windows bring natural light into every room and keep the whole house well-ventilated. The kitchen includes an electric stove, and the cedar doors carry the same warm detail through the rest of the home.

The bedrooms are spacious, each with its own walk-in closet and private bathroom. The master suite stands out for its fine colonial coffered ceiling, one of the architectural highlights of the house. At the front, a broad terrace opens onto panoramic views of the three volcanoes, a natural spot for quiet mornings and evening rest.

Set in a controlled-access, low density community, the home offers privacy without being far from the everyday services and surroundings of Antigua.

- **Living, dining and kitchen** in an open plan
- **Ceramic tile floors and high ceilings** throughout
- **Exposed cedar beams** and fine cedar doors

The asking price is US$475,000 plus taxes.

The home sits in a private residential community a short drive from the historic center of Antigua Guatemala, in a quiet area with green surroundings and striking views toward the volcanoes. It is set apart from the bustle of town while keeping everyday services within easy reach.
