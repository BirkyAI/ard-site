## About This Property

Villa Magna is a comfortable and elegant colonial home in one of the most exclusive residential areas just minutes from Antigua Guatemala. It takes a modern approach to the colonial style, working with exposed materials, open spaces and subtle traditional touches like tiled roofs and wooden coffered ceilings.

The heart of the home is a bright inner courtyard that brings natural light and fresh air through the whole house. The living, dining and kitchen areas open onto this space, and the kitchen stands out for its double height, its fully integrated cedar bar and its work surfaces finished in certified Brazilian granite. The kitchen is the most social room in the house, designed as a true gathering place.

Out on the upper level, four comfortable bedrooms each have their own private bathroom. The master suite and a second super master suite lead the way, with the latter offering a generous 40 square meters and an unmatched view of the Volcán de Agua. A charming terrace is perfect for enjoying the mild climate and watching the sunset in a serene setting.

The property is finished to a high standard, with colonial floors, high ceilings and unique architectural details that give every room elegance, character and distinction.
